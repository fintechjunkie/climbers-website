# The Oath Lords

Ten figures swore the Oath and were remade. **Not roster entities**, so
`npm run refs` neither creates this folder nor audits it, and nothing here
counts toward the `n/33` ready count.

Absalom · Cipher · Fawn · Malacus · Sister Silence · Thorne · Vesper Quill ·
Vex · Yuki, and **Grey**, who became the tenth fifteen months before the
Registration.

Two of the ten are not in this folder, because both have appeared in prose and
both were promoted under the rule below:

- **Grey** → `CHARACTERS/GREY/`. He narrates Prologue I and appears in all
  fourteen of its plates.
- **Sister Silence** → `CHARACTERS/SISTER_SILENCE/`. Prologue II spread 11.

So this folder holds the eight who have not yet appeared on a page.

## Promoting one

When an Oath Lord first appears in prose:

1. Add them to `content/roster.json` as a `CHARACTER`.
2. Run `npm run refs` to scaffold `CHARACTERS/<TOKEN>/`.
3. **Move** the image there under the filename that folder's README names.
4. Write their `block` **from the image**, not from memory.

Step 4 is not a formality. Sister Silence was promoted with a block written
without opening her file, and nearly every detail was wrong — plain robes
instead of gold-and-black vestments, a bare face instead of a worked gold band
across the eyes. The plate written against that block had to be redone. Look at
the art first.

## One of these is already load-bearing

**Absalom.** The Delivery's first plate is a close study of a sealed pouch with
**Absalom's skull sigil pressed into black wax** — the seal is the subject of
the image and must be sharp and central. He is also the one who took Rainbow's
right eye, which Prologue III spread 1 depends on.
