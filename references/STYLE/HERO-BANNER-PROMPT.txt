WHY THE CURRENT ONE IS BEING REPLACED
  It is a photoreal 3D city render with a generic glowing spire. It is not in
  the book's style, and it is not Haven City — it could be any science-fiction
  city. Every one of the 64 plates inside the site is screen-printed pulp
  illustration, so the first thing a reader sees should be too, and it should
  be a place they will recognise ten pages in.

>>> THIS IS THE ONE IMAGE IN THE ENTIRE PROJECT THAT CARRIES TEXT. <<<
  Every plate prompt bans lettering outright. This one requires it. The word
  CLIMBERS is part of the design, not an overlay added afterwards.

STYLE: CLIMBERS Pulp SciFi. The attached style reference is the authority for
palette, light, surface and finish. Reproduce it; do not reinterpret it.

Bold retro-futurist pulp science-fiction illustration. Vintage pulp sci-fi
poster energy with graphic-novel readability: screen-printed / lithographic
texture, visible print grain, bold graphic shadow masses, crisp ink-like edge
definition, strong silhouettes, selective detail rather than uniform micro-
detail, restrained and controlled luminous accents, angular futuristic
architecture, dramatic perspective, cinematic composition.

IT MUST READ AS ILLUSTRATED AND PRINTED. Not photorealistic, not a 3D render,
not a game screenshot, not glossy, not airbrushed, not hyper-detailed. If it
looks like a rendered cityscape rather than a printed poster, it has failed.

>>> THE SUBJECT: THE TOWER, SEEN FROM THE BOTTOM OF THE ROAD THAT LEADS TO IT. <<<
This is a story about people who walk into that building, so the picture is the
view they have before they decide. Camera at STREET LEVEL, low, looking straight
down the length of a broad approach road that runs away from us to the Tower.
Strong one-point perspective: the road, the kerbs, the building lines and the
reflections all drive toward the same point, and that point is the gate.

THE TOWER fills the upper centre of the frame and its top leaves the picture.
GOLD and FACETED, stepped back in setbacks like a needle built out of blades,
with ONE VERTICAL SEAM OF WHITE LIGHT running its full height. At its base, the
GATE: an arch of receding arches layered back like a cathedral, standing open,
filled with gold light so bright the inside of it is not readable. It has no
doors, no leaves, no hinges, nothing that could ever be shut.

THE CITY runs down both sides of the road, close and human-scaled against all
that height: blocky angular Haven City architecture in deep navy and warm stone,
balconies, walkways, cable runs, service gantries, stacked frontages, laundry
lines, and WINDOWS WITH PEOPLE'S LIVES BEHIND THEM — warm, occupied, ordinary.
Signage and displays throw MAGENTA, CYAN AND VIOLET across the wet road and
every one of those colours comes back up off it, doubled. Small hot points of
green and amber on junction boxes and door frames at intervals down both sides.
This is a lit, working, inhabited city at dusk — never a ruin, never abandoned,
never a wasteland.

Far off to one side and much lower, a soft BLUE MASS of light where the Valari
Quarter sits. Deep indigo sky going warm only where the Tower burns.

>>> ONE FIGURE, SMALL, WALKING TOWARD THE GATE. <<<
A single silhouette on the road at mid-distance, back to us, unhurried, maybe a
tenth of the frame height — small enough that the city dwarfs them and the Tower
dwarfs the city. No face, no detail, no gear, nobody identifiable, and NOT any
character from the story. We are behind them because the reader is meant to take
that place; this is the one picture in the project where a figure seen from
behind is correct.
NOBODY ELSE ON THE ROAD.

>>> THE TITLE: THE WORD "CLIMBERS", AND IT MUST BE SPELLED EXACTLY. <<<
C-L-I-M-B-E-R-S. One word. No subtitle, no tagline, no author name, no date, no
publisher mark, no URL, and NO OTHER TEXT ANYWHERE IN THE IMAGE — nothing on the
signage, nothing on the buildings, nothing on the road. The city's signs carry
abstract shape and colour only.

  SET IT ACROSS THE UPPER THIRD, in the open sky above the city and clear of the
  Tower's peak, centred and spanning most of the width.

  MODERN GEOMETRIC SANS-SERIF, ALL CAPITALS, WIDE LETTER-SPACING. Clean, flat,
  confident, engineered — the lettering of a transit sign or a spacecraft
  fuselage, not of a fantasy novel. Even stroke weight. No serifs.

  FLAT WARM CREAM-WHITE with a faint gold edge. It sits ON the picture as
  printed type.

  DO NOT give it: chrome, bevel, emboss, drop shadow, outer glow, neon tube
  treatment, gradient fill, distressed grunge, cracks, 3D extrusion, perspective
  distortion, motion blur, lens flare, or a box behind it. No sparkles. Nothing
  that would look dated in a year.

  It must be PERFECTLY LEGIBLE and correctly spelled, with even spacing and no
  malformed, doubled, missing or invented letters. If any letter is wrong, the
  whole image is unusable.

>>> COMPOSITION <<<
Upper third: sky and the title. Middle: the Tower and the gate burning at the
end of the road. Lower two thirds: the city crowding in on both sides and the
wet road carrying the colour back toward us.
The three value zones must all be present — near-black city masses in the
foreground, a midtone story field of streets and frontages, and the gate as the
bright focal region. Keep the lower-right corner free of anything load-bearing.

NO BORDER, NO FRAME, NO VIGNETTE, NO WATERMARK, NO SIGNATURE.
