REL'MIA — CANONICAL REFERENCE SHEET
====================================

===============================================================================
>>> DO NOT ATTACH NYX'ARA. THAT IS WHY IT KEPT COMING BACK LOOKING LIKE HER. <<<
===============================================================================

  Nyx'ara's canonical is attached to the Tale Two PLATES as a species reference,
  and it belongs there. It must NOT be attached to this sheet. An attached image
  outranks any amount of text: put a tall adult woman in front of the generator
  and ask for a seven-year-old, and you get a small Nyx'ara. That has been the
  single most expensive lesson of this whole project and it is exactly what is
  happening here.

  ATTACH ONLY THE TWO FILES BELOW. Nothing else. No Nyx'ara, no Sister Silence,
  no Sev'oran, no other Valari, no other plate.

STYLE: CLIMBERS Pulp SciFi. The attached style reference is the authority for
palette, light, surface and finish. Reproduce it; do not reinterpret it.

Bold retro-futurist pulp science-fiction illustration. Vintage pulp sci-fi
poster energy with graphic-novel readability: screen-printed / lithographic
texture, visible print grain, bold graphic shadow masses, crisp ink-like edge
definition, strong silhouettes, selective detail rather than uniform micro-
detail, restrained luminous accents. It must read as ILLUSTRATED AND PRINTED —
not photorealistic, not glossy 3D, not airbrushed.

>>> THE ATTACHED CROP IS THE PERSON. REPRODUCE HER. <<<
The girl in the attached crop IS this character. Reproduce her face, her
colouring, her hair, her build, her age and her clothing EXACTLY as they appear
there. Do not redesign her, do not age her up, do not stylise her differently,
do not improve her. The only things that change are the POSE, the ANGLE and the
BACKGROUND, which are specified below.

>>> WHAT TO CHANGE: TURN HER TO FACE THE VIEWER, AND EMPTY THE BACKGROUND. <<<
ONE FIGURE, FULL BODY, HEAD TO FEET, STANDING, FACING THE VIEWER SQUARE ON,
arms relaxed at her sides, weight even on both feet. In the crop she is in
profile; here she turns to face us.
Flat, even, neutral lighting with NO drama and NO colour cast — not the warm
gold of the crop, which came from a room she is no longer standing in.
PLAIN UNIFORM BACKGROUND: one flat neutral mid-tone. No room, no floor line, no
horizon, no counter, no props, no furniture, no scenery.
NOBODY ELSE IN FRAME. No adult, no hand on her shoulder, no figure behind her.
NO TEXT, NO LETTERING, NO LABELS, NO SWATCHES, NO BORDER, NO TURNAROUND PANELS,
NO MULTIPLE VIEWS. One figure, one pose, one picture.

>>> WHAT MUST BE REPRODUCED EXACTLY, BECAUSE IT IS ALREADY PRINTED <<<

  A VALARI GIRL, SEVEN YEARS OLD, AND UNMISTAKABLY A SMALL CHILD. Child
  proportions — head large for the body, short limbs, narrow shoulders, soft
  round face, no adult jawline, no adult figure, no adult height. She would come
  up to an adult's chest.

  SKIN: the deep cornflower-to-periwinkle blue of the crop, cool and even.

  EARS: long and pointed, clearly visible, swept clear of the hair.

  EYES: pale and luminous, large in a child's face.

  HAIR: DARK, VERY LONG, worn in MANY FINE BRAIDS gathered together and falling
  forward over her LEFT shoulder, down past her waist, with the hairline pulled
  back neatly off her face. Braided by a parent. This is her most identifying
  feature at a distance and it does not change between plates.

  CIRCUIT TRACERY: a FINE, PALE, CLOSE-SET HEXAGONAL MESH across the TEMPLE AND
  UPPER CHEEK on each side, and NOWHERE ELSE — nothing on the neck, nothing on
  the jaw, nothing on the arms or hands. Faint, low-contrast, drifting slowly
  between a soft blue and a dusty magenta-violet, both low-saturation. Never hot
  pink, never electric blue, never neon, never bright.

  CLOTHES: a simple SHORT-SLEEVED SHIFT DRESS in PALE SAND-GOLD, gathered at the
  waist, falling to mid-calf. Plain, clean, well-kept, chosen by a parent.
  Simple plain shoes. NO uniform, NO gown, NO robes, NO tech, NO jewellery, NO
  accessories, NO bag.

>>> SHE IS NOT NYX'ARA AND MUST NOT DRIFT TOWARD HER. <<<
This sheet has already failed once by producing a small version of an adult
Valari woman. The differences, stated plainly:
  - NYX'ARA IS A TALL ADULT. Rel'mia is a small child, and the PROPORTIONS have
    to say so before the colour does.
  - NYX'ARA'S CIRCUITS RUN THE FULL LENGTH OF HER NECK AND JAW. Rel'mia's stop
    at the temples and cheeks. Nothing below the face. This is the fastest way
    to tell them apart.
  - NYX'ARA'S HAIR IS WORN LOOSE AND DARK. Rel'mia's is in many fine braids
    gathered over one shoulder.
  - NYX'ARA WEARS ADULT CLOTHING WITH STRUCTURE. Rel'mia wears a plain sand-gold
    child's shift.
  - SISTER SILENCE has a gold band across her eyes and gold vestments. Rel'mia
    has neither.
If the finished sheet could be mistaken for a young adult woman, it is wrong.

>>> HER EXPRESSION IS THE MOST IMPORTANT THING ON THIS SHEET. <<<
She looks DIRECTLY AT THE VIEWER, level and steady, with OPEN ASSESSMENT — the
way a child looks at something they have not made their mind up about and have
not learned to hide that they are studying. Calm. Unhurried. Present. Faintly
curious. Entirely unafraid.

  NOT frightened. NOT sad. NOT tearful. NOT wide-eyed. NOT pleading. NOT
  cowering. NOT huddled. NOT shy. NOT smiling. NOT cute. NOT winsome.

  DO NOT MAKE HER PITIABLE. This is the failure to guard against above all
  others, because everything about her situation invites it. She is the most
  composed person in every scene she appears in, and the sheet has to establish
  that so it survives into the plates. If a viewer's first reaction is to feel
  sorry for her, the sheet has failed. The right reaction is to feel looked at.

  POSTURE MATCHES: standing straight and settled, hands loose at her sides. Not
  clutching herself, not fidgeting, not hunched, not holding anything.

Square, 1:1. No text, no lettering, no border, no caption, no watermark.
