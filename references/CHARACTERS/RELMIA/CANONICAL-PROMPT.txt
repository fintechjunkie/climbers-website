REL'MIA — CANONICAL REFERENCE SHEET
====================================

WHY THIS EXISTS
  She appears in THREE plates of Tale Two and she has no canonical, so she has
  been drifting — two attempts came back as a human child, and even with a
  species reference attached her hair, clothes and age were free to change from
  plate to plate. Sev'oran does not need one because she appears once. Rel'mia
  does.

WHY IT IS BUILT FROM A PLATE RATHER THAN INVENTED
  Spread 1 has already landed and she is clearly visible in it, standing at the
  intake desk between her parents. That plate is IN THE BOOK. If the canonical
  disagreed with it, the canonical would be wrong on arrival and every later
  plate would contradict a picture the reader has already seen.

  So the sheet is drawn FROM her, not instead of her.

STYLE: CLIMBERS Pulp SciFi. The attached style reference is the authority for
palette, light, surface and finish. Reproduce it; do not reinterpret it.

Bold retro-futurist pulp science-fiction illustration. Vintage pulp sci-fi
poster energy with graphic-novel readability: screen-printed / lithographic
texture, visible print grain, bold graphic shadow masses, crisp ink-like edge
definition, strong silhouettes, selective detail rather than uniform micro-
detail, restrained luminous accents. It must read as ILLUSTRATED AND PRINTED —
not photorealistic, not glossy 3D, not airbrushed.

>>> TAKE THE CHARACTER FROM THE ATTACHED SCENE, AND ONLY THE CHILD. <<<
In the attached picture, four figures stand at a counter. Take EVERYTHING from
the SMALL VALARI GIRL IN THE PALE DRESS on the left, the one with an adult's
hand resting on her shoulder. Reproduce her face, her colouring, her hair, her
build, her age and her clothing exactly.
IGNORE EVERYONE AND EVERYTHING ELSE IN THAT IMAGE — the two adults, the
Synthetic, the counter, the pool, the architecture, the warm light. None of it
appears here.

>>> THIS IS A REFERENCE SHEET, NOT A SCENE. <<<
ONE FIGURE, FULL BODY, HEAD TO FEET, STANDING, FACING THE VIEWER, arms relaxed
at her sides, in a plain neutral pose. Flat, even, neutral lighting with no
drama and no colour cast — NOT the warm gold of the attached scene. PLAIN
UNIFORM BACKGROUND: a single flat neutral mid-tone. No room, no floor line, no
horizon, no props, no furniture, no scenery, no second figure.
NO TEXT, NO LETTERING, NO LABELS, NO SWATCHES, NO BORDER, NO TURNAROUND PANELS,
NO MULTIPLE VIEWS. One figure, one pose, one picture.

>>> WHAT MUST BE REPRODUCED EXACTLY, BECAUSE IT IS ALREADY IN THE BOOK <<<

  A VALARI GIRL, SEVEN YEARS OLD, AND UNMISTAKABLY A SMALL CHILD. Child
  proportions — head large for the body, short limbs, soft face, no adult
  jawline. She stands about chest height to an adult. If she could be read as a
  teenager, the sheet is wrong.

  SKIN: the medium-saturation cornflower-to-periwinkle blue of the attached
  figure. Cool and even. Not teal, not turquoise, not grey, not purple.

  EARS: long and pointed, clearly visible, not covered by hair.

  EYES: pale and luminous, large in a child's face.

  HAIR: DARK, VERY LONG, worn in ONE THICK BRAID that falls forward over her
  LEFT shoulder and down past her waist, with the hairline swept back off her
  face. This is her single most identifying feature at a distance and it must
  not change between plates.

  CIRCUIT TRACERY: a FINE, PALE, CLOSE-SET MESH across the TEMPLE AND UPPER
  CHEEK on each side, and NOWHERE ELSE — nothing on the neck, nothing on the
  jaw, nothing on the arms or hands. It is FAINT and low-contrast, drifting
  slowly between a soft blue and a dusty magenta-violet, both low-saturation.
  Never hot pink, never electric blue, never neon, never bright.
  An adult Valari's tracery has spread down the neck and jaw over a lifetime.
  Hers has barely started, and that sparseness is how a reader reads her age.

  CLOTHES: a simple SLEEVELESS SHIFT DRESS in PALE CREAM-SAND, plain, softly
  gathered, falling to mid-calf. Clean, well-kept, unfashionable, chosen by a
  parent. Plain simple shoes.
  NO uniform, NO institutional gown, NO hospital clothing, NO robes, NO armour,
  NO tech, NO jewellery, NO accessories, NO bag, NO weapon.

>>> HER EXPRESSION IS THE MOST IMPORTANT THING ON THIS SHEET. <<<
She looks DIRECTLY AT THE VIEWER, level and steady, with OPEN ASSESSMENT — the
way a child looks at something they have not made their mind up about and have
not yet learned to hide that they are studying. Calm. Unhurried. Present.
Faintly curious. Entirely unafraid.

  NOT frightened. NOT sad. NOT tearful. NOT wide-eyed. NOT pleading. NOT
  cowering. NOT huddled. NOT shy. NOT smiling. NOT cute. NOT winsome.

  DO NOT MAKE HER PITIABLE. This is the failure to guard against above all
  others, because everything about her situation invites it. She is the most
  composed person in every scene she is in, and the sheet has to establish that
  so it survives into the plates. If a viewer's first reaction is to feel sorry
  for her, the sheet has failed. The right reaction is to feel looked at.

  POSTURE MATCHES: standing straight and settled, weight even on both feet,
  hands loose at her sides. Not clutching herself, not fidgeting, not hunched,
  not holding anything.

>>> WHAT SHE MUST NOT LOOK LIKE. <<<
Three other Valari women appear in this series and she must not drift toward
any of them:
  - NYX'ARA is a tall adult whose circuits run the full length of her neck and
    jaw. Rel'mia's stop at the temples.
  - SISTER SILENCE has very long loose dark hair and a gold band across her
    eyes. Rel'mia's hair is braided and her eyes are uncovered.
  - SEV'ORAN is a tired adult in worn indoor clothes.
She is a CHILD, and everything about the proportions must say so before the
colour does.
