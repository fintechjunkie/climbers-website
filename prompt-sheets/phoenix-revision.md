# PHOENIX — CANONICAL REVISION

**Attach:** that same file (the version you just made) + the Pulp SciFi guide.

## Why this is being re-run

The chassis, face, build and colour language are all good and should survive
the pass. Two things are wrong, and one of them is not cosmetic.

**The optical sensors read red.** The roster and both of his plate specs say
ORANGE. Bone Patrol officers have RED optics, and that is a distinction the
world is using — a Wellness Center intake clerk should not share an eye colour
with the security force. The suit accents are already the correct orange; the
eyes need to match them.

**He is armed, and he is a clerk.** Sidearm on the thigh, ammunition pouches on
the belt, knee armour, combat boots. Phoenix is an intake coordinator whose
whole characterisation is *"furniture with good posture."* The second plate in
Seven turns on a Sandman evaluating him with **no weapon and no lean-in** —
the menace in that Tale is bureaucratic, and an openly armed Synthetic changes
the power balance of the scene. The last plate has him holding a door open for
a seven-year-old, which reads very differently with a pistol on his leg.

## The prompt

> Revise the **attached character canonical**. Keep the CLIMBERS character
> library rendering language exactly as it is — same full-body framing, same
> neutral standing pose, same dark ground with the soft rim glow, same level of
> finish. This is a targeted edit, not a redesign.
>
> **KEEP EXACTLY AS THEY ARE:**
> - the head and face: the faceted white-and-grey plated skull, the smooth
>   plated cheeks and jaw, the segmented ear housing, the neck construction
> - the build, height and proportions
> - the dark charcoal-navy uniform, its panel seams and its collar
> - the segmented mechanical hands and forearms
> - the orange accent striping at the shoulders, collar, thighs, knees and boots
> - the pose: one hand raised across the chest, the other open at his side
> - the full-body framing, the dark ground, the rim glow
>
> **CHANGE — two things only:**
>
> 1. **The optical sensors become ORANGE**, matching the orange accent striping
>    already on the uniform. Warm amber-orange, clearly not red. This is his
>    single most identifying feature and it must not read as red under any
>    lighting.
>
> 2. **Disarm him completely. He is administrative staff, not security.**
>    Remove the sidearm and its thigh holster. Remove the ammunition and
>    utility pouches from the belt — leave a plain, simple belt with the small
>    orange buckle plate. Remove the armoured knee plates and the armoured
>    shin and boot plating; give him plain soft-soled service footwear in the
>    same charcoal-navy. Remove the thigh cargo pocket and the shoulder webbing.
>    Remove the wrist device with the red indicator, or change its indicator to
>    the same orange.
>
>    What is left should read as a **uniform, not a kit**: clean, well-fitted,
>    slightly formal, with nothing on it that could be used on a person. He
>    works at a desk in a building designed to look like medicine.
>
> His posture stays textbook-correct to the point of being a performance —
> that stiffness is the character and it should survive the disarming. He is
> not friendly and not threatening. He is impeccable.
>
> Full body head to feet, entire silhouette readable. No environment, no floor,
> no scenery, no text, no border, no frame, no nameplate, no UI overlays, no
> weapons of any kind.
